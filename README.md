# news-image-20261121 — The World in One Day (21 November 2026)

One clean front-page image summarising Saturday 21 November 2026's world news:
COP31 overtime in Antalya (fossil-fuel language dropped from the draft), the
worst week of 2026 for Wall Street as the AI trade melts (Nasdaq −5.3%,
SoftBank −16%), Russian missiles crashing inside Poland during NATO drills,
Iran downing US drones over Hormuz, the Kryvyi Rih strike on Zelenskyy's
hometown, and nine briefs across diplomacy, energy, health and the Americas.

Every claim was verified against same-day reporting from Reuters, AP,
Al Jazeera, The Guardian, DW, Axios, Bloomberg, PBS and Xinhua before it
went on the page.

## Run

```
node main.ts
```

The program validates the committed `entry.png` (PNG signature, 1024×1024,
≤1 MiB) and copies it byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
